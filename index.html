Collaborative Supervision for Practitioners

This supervision group provides a reflective, collegial space for psychotherapists and counsellors to deepen clinical understanding and professional growth.

Supervision Approach

The group emphasizes relational, integrative, and reflective practice models. Our supervision process draws upon psychodynamic and humanistic frameworks, offering a shared space for exploring clinical process, countertransference, and ethical dilemmas in practice.

Meetings are conducted monthly in small groups, fostering peer reflection and mutual learning among practitioners from diverse therapeutic backgrounds.
